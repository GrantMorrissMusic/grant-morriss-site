02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE SONGS

The long
way
round.

UK-born. Raised on records. Still chasing the feeling of finding the right song at exactly the right time.

My earliest music memory is getting the bus into town with my saved-up pocket money to go CD hunting. The first one I remember buying was Mansun’s Attack of the Grey Lantern. Then came Nevermind, Bleed American and From Under the Cork Tree, and I never really recovered.

These days I’m a dad with a proper job. I write, play and record everything myself in a spare room. The songs are personal, a bit autobiographical and occasionally surreal. Loud guitars, big hooks, and the belief that feelings sound better turned up.
